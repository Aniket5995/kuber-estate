Our process

Five steps.
No shortcuts.

Keep scrolling to walk through how every engagement runs — the same sequence whether it's a first home or a commercial portfolio.

01

Understand

A conversation about your goals, budget, timeline, preferred areas and what "right" looks like for you — self‑use, rental income or appreciation.

You getA written requirement brief
02

Shortlist

We match the brief against the market and our own high‑return criteria, then arrange site visits — only for properties worth your time.

You getA matched shortlist & site visits
03

Verify

Title, RERA registration, sanctioned plans and approvals, encumbrances, government records, dues and litigation — checked before any money moves.

You getA document verification report
04

Evaluate

Fair price against comparable deals, expected rental yield, appreciation outlook and the risks — assessed purely in your interest. If it doesn't hold up, we say so.

You getA price & return estimate
05

Finalise

We negotiate on your side, then manage the agreement, payment schedule, registration and possession — one point of contact until the keys are in your hand.

You getA closed deal, registered & handed over
