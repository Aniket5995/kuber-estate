The process

Five steps. No shortcuts.

The same sequence on every engagement — a first home, a commercial portfolio, a plot, a sale. Tracked in KuberCRM, so you always know where your deal stands.

  1. Step 01

    Understand

    A conversation about your goals, budget, timeline, preferred areas and what "right" looks like for you.

    You getA written requirement brief
  2. Step 02

    Shortlist

    We match the brief against the market and our own criteria, then arrange site visits — only for properties worth your time.

    You getA matched shortlist & site visits
  3. Step 03

    Verify

    Title, RERA registration, sanctioned plans and approvals, encumbrances, government records, dues and litigation — checked before any money moves.

    You getA document verification report
  4. Step 04

    Evaluate

    Fair price against comparable deals, expected yield, appreciation outlook and the risks — assessed purely in your interest. If it doesn't hold up, we say so.

    You getA price & return estimate
  5. Step 05

    Finalise

    We negotiate on your side, then manage the agreement, payment schedule, registration and possession — one point of contact until it's done.

    You getA closed deal, registered & handed over
