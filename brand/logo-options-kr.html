Kuber Estate · logo concepts

KR — three directions.

Every concept is one ligature: the K and the R share a spine and a leg. Each also carries the two ideas you asked for — real estate (a roof, a house, a building) and always growing, always giving back (an upward move, with the mint marking what comes back to the client). Solid black, one mint accent, works at favicon size.

KR 1 — Rise

Arrow · plot

K+R: one spine, the R's angular bowl, one shared leg. Growth & giving: the K's upper arm breaks out of the bowl and becomes an arrow — the mint head is the return that goes back to the client. Real estate: the bowl's counter is a roof pitch, and the whole mark stands on a ground line — the plot. Cleanest and most 'brand-like' of the three.
