KuberCRM.

Consulting, powered by our own software.

KuberCRM is the real estate CRM we built to run Kuber Estate — every enquiry, property, document and follow‑up in one place. It's why nothing falls through the cracks, and why we can tell you exactly where your deal stands, any day, from anywhere.

What it does

Everything a deal needs, in one place.

Requirement tracking

Every client's purpose, budget, areas and timeline captured once — and matched automatically against every new property that comes in.

Property & document vault

Title, approvals, verification reports and photos stored per property. Nothing lost in a chat, nothing unchecked.

Deal pipeline

Shortlist → visit → verify → negotiate → close. Every deal on one board, every stage visible to the whole team.

Client view

Clients see the same stages, documents and next steps you do — from anywhere in the world.

Follow‑ups & reminders

Site visits, callbacks, payment schedules and registration dates — nudged before they slip.

Reports

Enquiries by source, conversion by stage, time to close — the numbers a firm actually runs on.

Who it's for
  • Our own clients — every engagement runs on it
  • Brokerages and consultancies that want the same discipline
  • Developers tracking enquiries across projects
  • Teams tired of deals living in WhatsApp chats
Why we built it

We couldn't find a CRM that thought the way a property consultancy works — requirement first, documents per property, one pipeline from shortlist to keys. So we built one, ran our own firm on it, and kept what survived real deals. That's KuberCRM.

It's the reason our clients always know where their deal stands, and the reason nothing falls through the cracks between a site visit and a registration.
