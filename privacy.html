Privacy notice

How this site treats your data.

Short version: we don't track you, and we only receive what you choose to send us.

Cookies

Kuber Estate does not set tracking or advertising cookies. The site stores one small preference in your browser — whether you dismissed the privacy notice — so it doesn't ask again. No analytics scripts run on this site.

Third‑party content

Two things are loaded from Google: our web fonts (Google Fonts) and, on the project page, an embedded Google Map that appears only after you press Show map. Google may set its own cookies and receives your IP address when those load. See Google's privacy policy. If you decline the notice, the map stays hidden unless you choose to show it.

Enquiries

When you use the enquiry form, your details are composed into a WhatsApp message on your own device — nothing is stored on this site. Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram or email are used only to respond to your enquiry and are kept in our CRM for as long as we work with you.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which logs IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions about your data: info@kuberestate.co.in.
